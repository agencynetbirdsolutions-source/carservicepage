Common Questions

How do I book a service?

Tap any WhatsApp button on this page and send your car model with the issue. Our advisor confirms a slot with the expected time and cost.

Yes. Original or OEM approved parts only, with the invoice shared and the old parts handed back for your check.

A standard periodic service takes about four to six hours. Bigger repairs depend on parts availability and we tell you upfront.

No. We follow the manufacturer recommended schedule and keep a record of every job so your warranty terms stay protected.

Yes, in nearby areas on request. Mention it while booking on WhatsApp and we arrange a driver for your slot.

Which payments do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and cash. The final invoice is shared before any payment is collected.
